# Music

## Collaborative piano<span class="marginnote">Williams College<br>Fall 2024–present</span>

Most of my playing at Williams is with other people. I accompany voice lessons, studio recitals, senior recitals, and chamber music, and I've been the rehearsal pianist for the January opera workshops, including *Faust* and *Gianni Schicchi*. I've played more than fifty pieces this way, from Bach cantatas to Sondheim.

This year I accompany the Williams choir, and I accompany and help direct the gospel choir.^[In February 2026 I also played in the Berkshire Symphony Student Soloist Competition, accompanying arias by Donizetti and Puccini.] The full list of what I've played is on the [repertoire](/music/repertoire/) page.

## Solo piano

I've taken piano lessons and played on a piano recital every semester since I arrived, with pieces by Medtner, Bach, Chopin, and Myaskovsky. Right now I'm learning Schubert's Sonata in C Minor, D. 958, and Schumann's Fantasie in C Major, Op. 17.

## Writing and score videos

In 2023, mentored by Dr. Jennifer Undercofler, I wrote an [analysis of Samuil Feinberg's Third Piano Sonata](https://docs.google.com/document/u/1/d/e/2PACX-1vQ8GMyX2w-vpc1dlm-_AQc7ZsWmcMK1wvb6e_AqcWg2kYeq4Axrjr9NJPCGh8hs0_S-VkTPOuvsM7pN/pub): its structure, and its harmonic and motivic ties to Liszt, Chopin, and Scriabin.

On my [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@fuguehoppy6956/videos) I synchronize recordings of classical pieces with their scores, so you can follow along as you listen.^[2021–present.] Making those videos is also where the idea for my [practice tool](/technical/#music-practice-tool) came from.
